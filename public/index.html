Dedupe Lab · a delivery-semantics lab

Exactly-once is a consumer property, not a wire guarantee.

Twelve card charges over a link that eats two ACKs. Change the policy around the wire — never the wire — and watch who pays the price.

Educational model. A deterministic replay of one lossy link — the same two first-attempt ACKs drop on every run so the three policies can be compared on identical physics. Replays run against this demo's own API; nothing else leaves the page.

"Exactly-once delivery" is at-least-once wearing a dedupe store. The wire never changes across the three policies — only who cleans up afterwards does.
